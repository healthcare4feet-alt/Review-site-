Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about joining, verification and discounts.

What is BrandsWhoCare?
A free membership that gives private health professionals, carers and disabled people discounts from brands that care.
Who can join?
Private health professionals on a UK regulator's register, paid or unpaid carers, and disabled people of any age. See About us.
Is it really free?
Yes. Membership is free and always will be. We're funded by commission from brands.
How does verification work?
Health professionals give a registration number, which we check against the public register, usually instantly. Carers and disabled people upload one proof document, which a person reviews, usually within [X] working days.
Which registers do you check?
HCPC, NMC, GMC, GPhC, GDC, GOsC, GCC and Social Work England.
What proof can carers use?
A Carer's Allowance letter, a care agency or employer letter, or a local authority carer's assessment.
What proof can disabled people use?
A PIP, DLA or Attendance Allowance award letter, a Blue Badge, or similar proof.
How is my data kept safe?
We only use your information to check eligibility and run your membership, and we keep proof documents only as long as needed. Read our Privacy policy.
How do the discounts work?
Once verified, log in and unlock an offer. You'll get a code or link to use on the brand's own site.
Do you earn money from my purchases?
Yes. Some links earn us a commission through Awin or Commission Factory. You never pay more. See our Affiliate disclosure.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes, any time. Contact us and we'll close your account and delete your data, unless the law requires us to keep it.
How do I contact you?
Use our contact page or email [contact email].
