BrandsWhoCare

About BrandsWhoCare

BrandsWhoCare is a free membership that brings together brands that want to say thank you and the people who care for everyone else.

Who we're for

  • Private health professionals: nurses, therapists, dentists, physios and other practitioners working in private healthcare.
  • Carers: paid and unpaid carers supporting family, friends and clients every day.
  • Disabled people: of every age, with savings that make everyday life easier.

How it works

Join free, verify your eligibility, then unlock discounts on energy, food, fashion, health, travel, tech, days out and home.

Who runs BrandsWhoCare

BrandsWhoCare is run by Mark, its founder.

Our Story

Recognising the people who care for others.

Having served in the Armed Forces for 14 years before retraining as a podiatrist, I have spent much of my working life serving others and working with people from all walks of life.

Through my work as a podiatrist, visiting care homes and working with people in their own homes, I have met countless dedicated carers who make a real difference to people's lives every single day.

I have seen first-hand the challenging conditions in which care home staff work, as well as the pressures faced by home carers who often feel undervalued and unsupported despite the vital role they play in our communities.

One conversation in particular stayed with me. A carer explained that she couldn't access the reward and discount cards available to other professions because she didn't meet the eligibility criteria.

It made me question why the people who dedicate their working lives to caring for others should miss out on these benefits.

That conversation inspired Brands Who Care.

My vision is to build a platform that gives carers access to meaningful offers, discounts and rewards, helping them feel recognised, appreciated and valued.

By bringing together ethical, caring brands and the people who dedicate their lives to supporting others, I want to help bridge the gap between businesses that care about their communities and the carers who deserve greater recognition.

Whether someone works in a care home, provides care in people's homes or supports others in a different caring role, I believe their contribution deserves to be recognised.

Brands Who Care is more than a discount website. It is an opportunity to give something back to the people who give so much of themselves to others.

Because caring for others should count for something.

Our Mission

Smarter savings for people who care, powered by AI.

Our mission is to become the world's leading discount platform for people who care, combining valuable brand offers with innovative technology to make saving money easier, smarter and more accessible.

We aim to transform the way people discover discounts, compare deals and shop, using artificial intelligence to deliver personalised savings and, in the future, intelligent shopping agents supported by secure, modern payment technology.

We are future-orientated, people-focused and technology-driven. By continually embracing innovation, building trusted partnerships and putting our members first, we strive to help carers, disabled people and the wider community make their money go further.

How the offers work

Once you're verified, you can browse discounts by category and unlock them. Each offer takes you to the brand's own website, where you buy directly from the brand. The offers come through affiliate networks, mainly Awin and Commission Factory, or directly from brands. A brand appearing here does not mean it endorses us.

Free, with no hidden charges

Membership is free. There's no subscription, no fee to unlock offers and no card details needed to join.

How we earn money

When you use an offer and buy something, the brand may pay us a small commission through Awin or Commission Factory. That's how we stay free. You never pay more because of it. Full details are in our Affiliate disclosure.
